# Checked IA8 clamp texture family — 2026-10-10

The [fresh IA8 trial](HARDWARE_IA8_CLAMP_2026-10-10.md) stops on a
32 × 32 IA8/clamp descriptor after another observed DrawQuad return.
Extend the existing structural IA8 family to clamp on both axes as well as
repeat on both axes. Object/source addresses and legal slots remain variable;
admission checks every field and the complete tiled backing span.

Only exact word 0 values `0x190` (clamp/clamp) and `0x195` (repeat/repeat)
are admitted for IA8. Linear filters, disabled edge LOD, zero LOD/bias,
matching format/dimensions/tile count, no mipmaps, user data or TLUT remain
required. Mixed modes and mirror are refused. I4/RGB5A3 admission remains
clamp-only. The existing production load bridge forwards validated native
wrap modes and refreshes the host object on every load.

ASan/UBSan executes the production bridge and checked Memory slice in both
modes: 485 valid loads each, 4,362 diagnosed refusals headless and 4,396
rendered. Synthetic data covers both captured descriptor shapes, all eight
slots, variable dimensions and partial tiles, relocated object/source,
truncated spans, forbidden fields and tile-count overflow. Changing one
object from clamp to repeat and back verifies stable host allocation and
fresh native sampler parameters. Seams verify forwarding and preserved
CPU, descriptor and backing bytes; they do not execute the GPU.

A fresh GPU scene executes the pinned native IA8 decoder and clamp sampler.
UVs (1.25, -0.75) sample outside both axes; a tiled intensity pattern produces
black/white under clamp and the opposite under repeat. Same-address backing
refresh and separate capture/replay processes preserve the expected pixels
and byte-identical PNGs. The full existing GPU suite, including repeat and
production DrawQuad scenes, passes. Private variants substituting repeat for
clamp and clamp for repeat are both rejected by independent pixel oracles.
These scenes exercise native decoding/sampling; the separate production
bridge contracts verify descriptor admission and wrap forwarding.

The separate private rendered build and complete successful published
exact-HEAD rollup are required before merge and SD deployment. Corrected
console execution and later pixels/playability remain pending. Private NROs,
game data, translated callers and raw reports remain excluded from GitHub.
